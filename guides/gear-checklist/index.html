Riding Gear Checklist

Everything to wear before the first ride.

Must have

  • DOT full-face helmet that fits snug
  • Goggles
  • Gloves
  • Long sleeves and long pants
  • Over-the-ankle boots

Nice to have

  • Chest protector
  • Knee and elbow guards
  • Neck brace for faster riders

Ride smart

wear a helmet and gear every ride, check your local laws and age rules before you buy, and younger riders need adult supervision.
