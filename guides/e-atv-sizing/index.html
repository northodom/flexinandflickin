Electric ATV Sizing by Age and Height

A quick way to match a quad to the rider.

Rough guide

Ages 6 to 10: small 24V to 36V quads with a parent speed limiter.

Ages 10 to 14: larger 36V to 48V youth quads, if the rider is inside the listed height and weight range.

Always follow the maker's listed age and weight limits over any chart, this one included.

Fit check

The rider should reach the bars and footrests comfortably and be able to work the brakes with a firm grip.

Ride smart

wear a helmet and gear every ride, check your local laws and age rules before you buy, and younger riders need adult supervision.
