How to Pick an Electric Dirt Bike

Fit, power and brakes, in that order.

1. Fit first

The rider should touch the ground with the balls of both feet. Check the listed seat height and max rider weight.

2. Power that matches skill

New riders do best with multiple speed modes and a lower top speed. More watts is not always better.

3. Brakes and suspension

Hydraulic disc brakes and adjustable suspension add control on rough ground.

4. Battery and support

Look for a removable battery, a clear warranty, and a seller that answers questions.

5. Rules

Check local laws, age rules and where you can legally ride before you buy.

Ride smart

wear a helmet and gear every ride, check your local laws and age rules before you buy, and younger riders need adult supervision.
